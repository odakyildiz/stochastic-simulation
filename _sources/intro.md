# Stochastic Simulation

This is an online companion to the lecture notes of the course Stochastic Simulation taught at Imperial College London, Department of Mathematics, by [O. Deniz Akyildiz](https://odakyildiz.com).

These pages feature extra explanations and code. Here we aim at reproduce almost every example in the notes in terms of implementation and results.

The lecture notes for the 2025-2026 (Spring) term will soon be posted (and it will be different than the last year -- so please do not use the existing notes for 2024-2025).

The table of contents for this page **will also be updated once the new notes are posted**.

Please note that this is a work in progress. If you find any errors, please let me know.
